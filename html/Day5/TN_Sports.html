Sports

Kabbadi

Kabaddi, known locally as Sadugudu stands as a testament to the spirit of teamwork and physical prowess. This contact sport involves two teams each taking turns raiding the opponent’s territory while the other team attempts to tag them. The game requires quick reflexes, strategic thinking and the ability to anticipate opponent’s moves making it a thrilling spectacle that has endured through the ages.

Silambam

Silambam, a traditional Indian martial art originating in Tamil Nadu showcases the grace and agility of its practitioners. Using a bamboo-stick staff participants engage in a rhythmic dance of strikes and blocks creating a mesmerizing display of fluid movements. Silambam not only serves as a means of physical fitness but also embodies the ancient martial traditions that have been preserved for centuries.

Jallikattu

Jallikattu, the famed bull-taming sport takes center stage during the Pongal festival in Tamil Nadu. Participants display courage and skill as they attempt to grab the hump of a bull and hold on to it. This ancient sport is deeply rooted in the agricultural traditions of the region and continues to be a symbol of bravery and valour.

Uriyadi

Uriyadi, a unique and colourful sport involves participants forming human pyramids to break a pot filled with coloured water suspended in the air. Often played during festivals, Uriyadi is a celebration of community spirit and collective effort. The joyous atmosphere and the vibrant display of teamwork make it a cherished tradition.
