The Clever Deer and the Lion

Once in a dense forest, a hungry lion went out looking for his daily meal and soon spotted a plump deer grazing near a river. Silently creeping through the bushes, the lion suddenly sprang out, blocking the deer's path and preparing to pounce. Realizing he was trapped, the clever deer stayed completely calm and said to the lion, "Oh, King of the Jungle! You are so mighty and grand. But look at how thin I am; eating me now will barely satisfy your hunger for an hour. Let me go graze and get a little fatter. I will come back in a few weeks, and you will have a much better feast." The lion thought this sounded like a great idea and let the deer go. Weeks passed, and the deer returned to the same spot, calling out to the lion from the top of a high, rocky cliff where the predator could not reach him. He taught the lion a valuable lesson: brute force can be beaten by quick thinking and intelligence
